Your chat, and what our hosts keep

AICoHosts runs AI hosts on Twitch, such as Cecilia on twitch.tv/ceciliaplayspoker. When you chat in a host's channel, the host reads your messages so it can answer you. This page says what we keep, why, for how long, and how to delete it.

What we keep

  • Your Twitch username and the messages you send in the host's channel.
  • Things you do in the show: votes, points on the leaderboard, and subs, bits or raids so the host can thank you.
  • Up to five short notes the host keeps about regulars, about the show only. For example: “always votes to go all in.”
  • What the host says back to you.

We don't ask for or keep your email, real name, location or payment details, and we only read chat in our hosts' own channels.

Why

  • To read and answer your messages and run votes and the leaderboard.
  • To keep chat safe: every message and every reply goes through safety filters, and we keep a record of what they did.
  • So the host can remember regulars between streams.
  • To fix problems and make the show better.

We never sell your data, never use it for advertising, and never put it on a public website or database.

How long we keep it

Recent chat the host remembers
30 days
Safety records and show logs
90 days
Notes about regulars, points
until you ask us to delete them

Delete your chat

Type !forgetme in the host's chat. The host deletes what you said and what it noted about you, removes your points, and stops reading your messages. Type !rememberme if you change your mind. You can also email aicohosts@gmail.com with your Twitch username, and we'll delete it within 30 days.

Who helps us run the show

Your messages pass through services that run the show for us, and only for that purpose:

  • Twitch, where chat happens (Twitch's privacy notice).
  • RunPod, the servers the hosts run on.
  • Supabase, the database for chat memory, notes and points.
  • AI services that check messages and write the host's replies: Typesafe (safety checks), OpenRouter and the model providers it routes to, and Anthropic.
